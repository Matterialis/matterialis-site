01

Where it started

Amphico has developed PFAS-free and toxic-free alternatives for the textile sector since 2018. Every project meant the same work: find candidate materials, screen them, sample them, test them, and watch most of them fail late.

02

The slow part

Materials R&D does not compress easily. A promising candidate can take months to source and qualify, and the right one is often sitting in another industry's catalogue under a name nobody in textiles would search for. Time to market was measured in years.

03

Tools for ourselves

So the team started building internal tools to speed up its own R&D, beginning with chemical space exploration: a way to map materials by what they do rather than where they are sold, and to predict the properties a datasheet leaves out.

04

Becoming Matterialis

Those tools outgrew the lab. With the support of a UKRI Future Leaders Fellowship they became Matterialis, a foundation model for industrial materials, built by the company that needed it first.
