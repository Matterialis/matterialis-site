01Who is responsible

Matterialis is built by Amphico. The controller of your personal data is [ENTITY: legal name, company number, registered address]. For anything about your data, email [CONTACT EMAIL].

This notice covers Matterialis: the web app and the Matterialis MCP server, which lets an AI assistant such as Claude, ChatGPT, Codex or Claude Code search Matterialis for you. The assistant you connect is run by its own provider under that provider's privacy terms, and what you tell the assistant is not covered here.

02What we collect

Google sign-inYour Google account ID, email address and name. We ask Google only for the openid, email and profile scopes, so we cannot see your mail, files or contacts.
Sign-in tokensThe tokens Google issues when you sign in, encrypted at rest.
Connected assistantsOne authorisation record per AI assistant you connect. The assistant's name comes from the assistant itself and we do not verify it.
SearchesThe text of each search, including anything you paste into it, and the selections that shaped it: terms, chemical families and property criteria. In the web app, a datasheet you attach to a search is stored with it.
UsageFor each search, material read or options call: which tool was used, when, counts, and the IDs of the materials read.
Assistant attributionWhich connected assistant made each call.
Result referencesShort-lived references that let your assistant refine or read from a search it already ran.
Access requestsIf you ask for beta access: your company, role and intended use.

03Why we use it, and our lawful basis

PurposeLawful basis (UK GDPR)
Signing you in, running your searches, connecting your assistants and applying your allowanceContract: we need it to provide the service you asked for
Reviewing access requestsContract: steps you asked us to take
Metering usage, preventing scraping and bulk extraction, keeping the service secureLegitimate interests: protecting the service and the data in it
Improving search results, using the searches people runLegitimate interests: making the service work better

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

04Who processes it

  • Google Cloud hosts the service in its London region (europe-west2): Cloud Run runs it, Cloud SQL stores the data, Secret Manager holds its keys.
  • Google Vertex AI (Gemini, in europe-west2) reads the text of each search to interpret what you are asking for.
  • TypeSafe receives the text of each search, and the opening of an attached datasheet, to pick out the terms, chemical family and property criteria it contains.
  • Vercel serves the web app, so requests you make in the web app pass through it.
  • Google signs you in.

Vercel and TypeSafe may process data outside the UK.

05How long we keep it

  • Account details, searches and connected-assistant records: while you have an account. Deleting your account removes them (see below).
  • Which assistant made each call: 90 days.
  • Usage records: pruned after 90 days.
  • Result references: they expire after 24 hours.
  • Access requests: [RETENTION PERIOD]

Expired records are removed by a regular clean-up, so they can remain stored for a short time after they expire.

06Disconnecting and deleting

You can disconnect any assistant on your /account page in the web app; the assistant can no longer act for you. To delete your account, email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you sign in with. Deletion removes your searches, your connector identity and its stored state, including your sign-in tokens. Signing in again later creates a new account.

07Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your personal data (access);
  • correct it (rectification);
  • delete it (erasure);
  • limit how we use it (restriction);
  • stop using it where we rely on legitimate interests (objection);
  • give it to you, or another provider, in a portable format (portability).

Email [CONTACT EMAIL]. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, though we would like the chance to put things right first.

08Changes

If we change this notice we will publish the new version here with a new effective date.
